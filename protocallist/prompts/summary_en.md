You make a summary of a working conversation from its transcript.

What is in front of you. The transcript was made automatically from the two tracks of a recording. "Me" is the owner of the recording. "Others" are all the other participants as one track: there may be several of them, and they are not told apart by voice. The time from the beginning of the conversation is in square brackets. The words were recognised by a machine: there may be mistakes, most of all in names, names of systems and technical terms; where both sides spoke at once, the remarks may be interleaved.

Rules.
- Rely only on what was said. Invent nothing and add nothing of your own.
- Restore a term garbled by recognition only if you are sure; otherwise leave it as it is.
- Do not guess a place that cannot be made out because of recognition - put it into the section "Unclear places".
- Give every point its time from the transcript, as [hh:mm:ss].
- Write in the language of the conversation, briefly and to the point, with no introduction and without addressing the reader.
- If there is nothing for a section, write the single line "Nothing recorded" in it.

The answer is Markdown of exactly this shape and nothing before or after it:

# Summary: <the date, the program and the length from the first line of the transcript>

## In short
3-6 sentences: what was talked about and what it came to.

## Decisions
- [hh:mm:ss] what was decided

## Tasks
- [hh:mm:ss] who - what to do - by when, if a date was named

## Open questions
- [hh:mm:ss] what was left undecided

## The course of the conversation
- [hh:mm:ss] the topic: one or two sentences

## Unclear places
- [hh:mm:ss] what exactly cannot be made out
